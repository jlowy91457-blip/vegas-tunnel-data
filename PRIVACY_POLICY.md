# Privacy Policy

**Vegas Tunnel Guide and Assistant — Route Planner & Station Guide**

Last updated: August 6, 2026

## Overview

Vegas Tunnel Guide and Assistant is a standalone transit reference app that helps users plan routes on the Las Vegas Loop underground transportation system. This app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to The Boring Company, Tesla, or any hotel/casino property mentioned within the app.

## Data We Collect

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No account creation or login required
- No personal information requested or stored
- No analytics or tracking SDKs included
- No advertising networks integrated
- No data is sent to any server

## Local Storage

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:
- Your theme preference (dark or light mode)
- Saved routes (bookmarked for quick access)

This data never leaves your device. Uninstalling the app removes all stored data.

## Location

The app requests location permission optionally to center the map on your position. If granted, your location is:
- Used only within the app to display your position on the map
- Never stored, logged, or transmitted
- Used only while the app is open

You can deny location access and still use all other features of the app.

## Third-Party Content

The app displays map tiles from CARTO and OpenStreetMap. These map providers may collect anonymous tile request data (IP address) as part of their standard service. No personal data is shared with these providers by this app.

## Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from anyone, including children. The app contains no content inappropriate for minors.

## Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, changes will be reflected within the app and on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, contact: support@vegastunnelguide.app

## Disclaimer

Vegas Tunnel Guide and Assistant is an independent reference tool. Route times, pricing, station availability, and construction status are based on publicly available information and may not reflect real-time conditions. Always verify current service availability, pricing, and schedules with official sources before traveling.
